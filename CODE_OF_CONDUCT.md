# 社区行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 中文

Clopen 社区欢迎不同经验、背景和能力的参与者。请保持尊重、具体和善意：讨论代码与行为，不攻击个人；尊重隐私；不骚扰、歧视、威胁或发布他人私人信息；接受维护者为保护社区所做的合理管理决定。

不当行为可通过仓库的私密安全报告渠道提交。维护者可以编辑、隐藏或删除不当内容，并对持续或严重违规者限制参与。执行决定会结合上下文、影响和重复程度。

## English

The Clopen community welcomes contributors of different experience levels, backgrounds, and abilities. Be respectful, specific, and constructive: discuss code and behavior rather than attacking people; respect privacy; do not harass, discriminate, threaten, or publish another person's private information; and accept reasonable moderation decisions made to protect the community.

Misconduct may be reported through the repository's private security-reporting channel. The maintainer may edit, hide, or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation for repeated or severe violations. Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact, and recurrence.
